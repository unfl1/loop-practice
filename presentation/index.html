Harness Engineering vs Loop Engineering

Harness = 작업 환경

AI가 안정적으로 행동할 수 있는 조건

도구, 파일, 테스트, 브라우저, 로그를 갖춘 실행 무대다.

Loop = 반복 과정

AI가 결과를 보고 다음 행동을 바꾸는 구조

평가, 피드백, 수정, 재생성을 반복하는 개선 흐름이다.

아직 어려운 부분

The ears became too round and bear-like; the reference ears are still small triangular ears with rounded corners and visible inner ear shape.

The head is now too wide and cloud-like compared with the reference's more balanced rounded-square fluffy head.

The eyes are a little too large and high for the reference, and the nose is slightly too centered in a cartoon muzzle.

The body still lacks the reference's subtle vertical puppy posture and soft taper from head to front legs.
