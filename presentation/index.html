Result Analysis
Best-of-N이 보여주는 것

          

            
Best에서 맞은 요소
Large head and compact body are closer to the reference puppy proportions.
Small triangular ears are lower and more embedded in the fluffy head outline.
Overall centered seated composition remains clear.
Line drawing style stays simple and intentionally non-realistic.

            
아직 어려운 요소
Face is still too circular and simplified compared with the slightly tapered lower muzzle area in the reference.
Eyes are too low and evenly spaced; the reference eyes sit a little higher within the face mass.
Nose is too rounded and large relative to the tiny puppy muzzle.
Side silhouette is symmetrical, while the reference has a slightly asymmetric seated body and paw placement.

          

          
Iteration score는 흔들릴 수 있지만 Best-so-far는 실제 최고 결과를 유지한다. 그래서 다음 생성 기준이 덜 흔들린다.
